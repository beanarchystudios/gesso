# Gesso

A Canvas client for schoolwork. Course material lives in Canvas. Personal writing does not.

## Language

**Notebook**:
Personal notes that belong to the student, not to any course.
_Avoid_: Scratchpad, Pages

**Pages**:
A course's wiki on Canvas.
_Avoid_: Notebook, Scratchpad

**Page**:
One wiki page in a course.
_Avoid_: Notebook page
